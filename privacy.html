Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

At Canela Beauty, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Information We Collect

When you place an order or create an account, we collect:

  • Payment information (processed securely by Stripe — we never store card details)

How We Use Your Information

  • To process and fulfill your orders

  • To send occasional promotional emails (you can unsubscribe anytime)

How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with trusted service providers who help us operate our business:

Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your information. All payments are processed through Stripe, which is PCI-DSS compliant. Your password is encrypted and never stored in plain text.
